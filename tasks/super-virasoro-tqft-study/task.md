# The Study on Super-Virasoro TQFT

> Draft placeholder: this contribution records a research direction and is not
> yet a merge-ready benchmark task.

Study Super-Virasoro TQFT as an advanced theoretical-physics problem. Develop a
self-contained account of the theory, its mathematical data, its physical
interpretation, and the concrete checks needed to distinguish a consistent
construction from a suggestive analogy.

The eventual task should ask the agent to read the relevant literature, state
the conventions it uses, formulate a precise research question, derive or
compute a nontrivial result, and present the result together with assumptions,
checks, limitations, and reproducible research artifacts. The final scientific
scope may include superconformal, graded, or spin-refined structures, but this
placeholder intentionally does not prescribe a particular model, relation, or
claimed theorem.

The exact frozen sources, research environment, output contract, oracle,
verifier, mentor package, and difficulty protocol will be specified in a later
revision after the scientific question is discussed with the maintainers.
